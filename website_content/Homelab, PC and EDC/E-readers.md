#### Xteink X4 

This is actually a magnificent little device. It runs on an STM32 so there are multiple firmwares for it. I have been using [Crosspoint](https://github.com/crosspoint-reader/crosspoint-reader) from day one and I find it perfect for the job. The magsafe feature is neat, but unfortunately doesn't work with the iPhone 13 mini. All in all it's a perfect **field reader**, just as I intended it to be in this setup.
