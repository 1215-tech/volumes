### Korean BBQ Shotgun Shells
==Online== 

**Ingredients**
- A pack of Manicotti
- A pack of bacon
- Ground beef and pork, or a store-bought mix, around a kilo
- 1 handful of green onions
- 1/4cup of canned carrots, can also be substituted with bell peppers

- 1 table spoon of sesame oil 
- 1 table spoon of chili oil
- 2 table spoons of Gochujang
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 table spoon ginger
- 3 table spoons of BBQ Rub
- 4 table spoons of BBQ Sauce

**Spicy Korean Mayo Ingredients**
- 1/2 Cup Mayo
- 2 table spoon Gochujang

- 1 lemon

### In-n-Out BBQ Onion Rings 
==Online== 

**Ingredients**
- ~500g ground beef
- 6 onions
- 2 table spoons of butter
- 1 table spoon of Worcestershire
- Salt, pepper and garlic to taste or 2 table spoons
- 1/2 cup chopped pickles
- 230g of cheddar
- 24 bacon slices
- 4 table spoons of BBQ rub
- 6 tablespoons of BBQ sauce
- Sesame seeds

**Optional In-n-Out Sauce**
- 1/2 cup of mayo
- 3 table spoons of ketchup or tomato paste 
- 2 table spoons of sriracha
- 2 table spoons of relish
- 1 table spoon of apple cider vinegar
- 1 tea spoon of sugar
- 2 table spoons of water

**Recipe**
- Cut ends of Onions and slice in the center and use a big and smaller ring. These will be used for the rings themselves
- Using the rest of the onion you did not use. Chop about 1 cup and sauté in a pan with 2 Tbsp Butter and 1 Tbsp Worcestershire for about 8 minutes until soft
- In a bowl add Ground Beef, Sautéed Onions, Seasonings, Pickles, Cheddar Cheese, and mix well
- Stuff in the mixture and use 2 slices of bacon to cover the rings
- Season with your favorite BBQ Rub
- In the Smoker or oven at 120 degrees for about 1.5 hours
- Then baste with BBQ and top with sesame seeds and back in the smoker for about 15 minutes

### Ezogelin
==Online== 

**Ingredients**
- 2 table spoon olive oil  
- 1 table spoon butter  
- 2 medium onion, diced  
- 8 cloves garlic, minced  
- 2 table spoons Turkish red pepper paste (biber salçası)  
- 2 table spoons tomato paste  
- 1 table spoons dried mint  
- 1 tea spoon ground cumin  
- 1 tea spoon paprika  
- Red pepper flakes (to taste)  
- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed and drained  
- 1/3 cup coarse bulgur, rinsed and drained  
- 1/4 cup short-grain rice, rinsed and drained  
- Salt & pepper to taste  
- Chicken stock or water as needed

**Spiced butter**
- 1/3 stick of butter  
- 1 table spoon of mint  
- 1 table spoon of paprika  
- 1 table spoon of Turkish red pepper paste  
- salt to taste

**Recipe**
- In a large soup pot, saute onions and garlic until fragrant before adding in both your tomato paste and Turkish red pepper paste
- Add your lentils, bulgur, and short-grain rice and mix. Add in chicken stock until the pot is filled. let simmer for 30–40 minutes, or until the grains are soft and fully cooked and the soup has thickened. Make sure to stir occasionally
- While this is going, make your spiced butter. Just combine everything under “spiced butter” and whisk together until combined
- Serve your Ezogelin soup with a squeeze of lemon juice, and a drizzle of the spiced butter and enjoy

### Crispy Beef Tacos
==Online== 

**Ingredients**
- 450-500g of ground beef (a mix can be used, preferably 80/20)
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 table spoons of tomato paste
- 1/2 cup beef stock
- 1 tablespoon of oil 

**Taco seasoning**
- 1 tablespoon cumin
- 1 tablespoon chili powder
- 2 tea spoons paprika
- 2 tea spoons oregano
- 2 tea spoons garlic powder
- 2 tea spoons black pepper
- 1 tea spoon cayenne (optional)

**Garlic lime Aioli**
- 1/2 cup of sour cream
- 1/2 cup mayo 
- 1 small jalapeno, deseeded (optional)
- 1.5 cups of cilantro (leaves + stems)
- 4-5 garlic gloves
- 2 tea spoons black pepper
- 1 tea spoon salt
- Juice of 1 lime

**For the tacos themselves**
- 10 to 12 mini tortillas
- 2 cups of shredded mozzarella
- Oil for frying

**Recipe**
- In a small bowl, whisk together the homemade taco seasoning spices to create a blend
- Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the diced onion and saute until soft and fragrant, about 3–4 minutes. Add the minced garlic and cook for another 30 seconds. Add the ground beef and break it up. Cook until mostly browned. Add the taco seasoning and tomato paste, mix well, and saute for 2–3 minutes. Pour in the beef stock and cook for 8–10 minutes, or until the liquid evaporates and the beef is saucy and fully cooked. Set aside
- In a blender or food processor add all of the aioli ingredients. Blend until smooth and green
- Lay a tortilla flat. Add a thin layer of mozzarella, 2–3 tbsp of the beef filling, then another sprinkle of cheese. Fold it over. Repeat until you’re done
- Heat a light layer of oil in a pan over medium-low heat. Add the folded tacos and cook until golden and crispy on both sides, adding more oil as needed. Work in batches so they crisp
- Plate them up, drown them in the garlic lime aioli, add extra cilantro (optional)

### Chicken Fajitas 
==Online== 

**Chicken marinade**
- 800g chicken thighs, sliced
- 3 table spoons mayo
- 2 table spoons oil
- 2 table spoons of honey and juice of 1 lime
- 7 garlic cloves, minced
- Seasonings: 2 table spoons paprika, 1 table spoon chili powder, 1 table spoon cumin, 1 tea spoon cayenne (optional), 2 tea spoons oregano, 2 tea spoons onion powder, 2 tea spoons garlic powder, 2 tea spoons salt and 2 tea spoons pepper

**Vegies**
- 1/2 red onion, sliced
- 1/2 each of red, yellow, orange and green bell pepper, sliced
- 2 tablespoons of oil
- Veggie seasonings (1 tea spoons each): garlic powder, cumin, chili powder, paprika, oregano, onion powder, salt, pepper

**Roasted garlic**
- 1 head of garlic, top sliced off + 1 table spoon of oil and 1 tea spoon of salt

**Roasted garlic chipotle sauce**
- 1/2 cup sour cream + 1/4 cup of mayo
- Roasted garlic from above
- 2 chipotle pepper + 1 table spoon honey + Juice of 1/2 lime

**Recipe**
- Add the sliced chicken to a bowl with mayo, oil, honey, lime juice, garlic, and all chicken seasonings. Toss until everything is coated. Marinate for at least a few mins, or up to overnight. Longer = better flavor
- Preheat oven to 220°C. Drizzle the garlic head with oil and salt, wrap it in foil, and place on a tray. Roast for 45–60 minutes, until soft & golden
- Add all sliced veggies to a large sheet pan. Drizzle with avocado oil, sprinkle with veggie seasonings, and toss to coat. Add the marinated chicken directly onto the pan with the veggies. Toss everything together so it plays nice. Bake for 20–25 minutes, until the chicken is fully cooked, lightly charred, and the veggies are tender
- Once the garlic is cool, squeeze the roasted cloves into a blender or food processor. Add sour cream, mayo, chipotle peppers, honey, and lime juice. Blend until smooth
- Spoon pan juices back over the chicken. Top with cilantro, extra lime, and dollops of the chipotle sauce
- Serve with wraps or tortillas

### Chicken Gyro
==Online==

**Chicken**
- 9 boneless and skinless thighs
- 3 table spoons olive oil 
- 5 cloves of garlic, minced
- 1/2 red onion, fully chopped
- 1/2 lemon juice
- 1/3 cup plain greek yogurt
- lemon zest (optional)

**Seasonings**
- 1 table spoon cumin
- 2 tea spoons smoked paprika
- 1 tea spoon oregano
- 1 tea spoon salt 
- 1.5 tea spoon garlic powder
- 1 tea spoon black pepper 

**Tzatziki**
- 1 cup plain greek yogurt
- 1 table spoon fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tea spoon salt 
- 1/4 cup grated cucumber
- 1 table spoon lemon juice
- 2 garlic heads (roasted 2 table spoons olive oil 2 tea spoons salt)
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

**Salad**
- 12 cherry tomatoes, cut in half
- 1/2 red onion, sliced
- 1 table spoon olive oil
- 1 tea spoon salt

**Wrap**
- Pitas
- Fries (optional)
- Parsley, finely chopped (optional)

**Recipe**
- Preheat oven to 220°C. Pat your chicken dry, then toss it in a bowl with olive oil, garlic, red onion, lemon juice, zest, and seasonings. Mix well and marinate for as long as your patience allows. Preferably overnight
- Halve an onion and place one half flat-side down on a baking dish (your DIY rotisserie base). Skewer it, then stack chicken high like a delicious Jenga tower. Top with red onion and a tiny foil hat to protect the peak. Roast 75–90 minutes, until golden, juicy, and slightly charred
- For roasted garlic, slice the tops off, drizzle with oil and salt, wrap in foil, and roast 35–40 minutes until soft and caramelized 
- Mix yogurt, dill, cucumber, lemon juice, olive oil, and salt. Squeeze in roasted garlic, mash, and stir until smooth. Chill until showtime
- Toss together cherry tomatoes, red onion, olive oil, and salt. Let them mingle for 5–10 minutes
- Once the chicken’s done, rest it 5 minutes, then slice thinly
- Assemble the wrap: pita, tzatziki, chicken, tomato salad, fries, and a parsley

### Beef Gyro
*This one is also simpler*

==Online==

**Ingridients**
- Ground beef 400g
- Fresh tomatoes 250g
- Red onion (to taste)
- Lavash or pita, 2 pitas
- Spices: paprika, chili powder, black pepper, dried garlic, salt - 1 each tea spoon each. 

**Tzatziki**
- Greek yogurt 200g
- Grated cucumber - 3 table spoons
- Garlic - 2 whole heads
- Mint - 1 tea spoon
- Dill - 1 table spoon
- Olive oil - 2 table spoons
- Salt and pepper - to taste 
- Lemon juice - table spoons

**Recipe**
- Preheat the oven to 200°C
- Place the ground beef on a sheet of parchment paper, cover with another sheet, and roll it out into an even thin layer. For a standard sheet, use about 350-400g of meat, leaving a little space at the bottom edge. Remove the top sheet of parchment and roll the meat into a tight log
- Transfer the meat roll to a baking sheet. Add the whole garlic heads and tomatoes to the same sheet. Lightly drizzle the garlic with olive oil
- Bake for 40 minutes at 200°C
- When done, remove from the oven and let everything cool for about 15 minutes
- While it cools, make the tzatziki: Squeeze the roasted garlic cloves out of their skins and mash them. Mix the Greek yogurt with the mashed roasted garlic, dill, mint, grated cucumber (finely grated and well squeezed to remove excess liquid), lemon juice, and olive oil. Season with salt and pepper to taste, then stir or blend until smooth
- Remove the cooled meat from the parchment and tear or slice it into small pieces
- Assemble the wraps: Spread tzatziki on a flatbread, add fresh cucumber slices (if using), the meat pieces, sliced red onion, and roasted tomatoes (peel the skins off first). Roll up tightly

### Soldiers Steak and Eggs
==Online==

**Ingredients**
- Beef steak. Any cut to be honest
- Eggs
- Olive oil, beef tallow or lard
- Butter
- Garlic and green onions
- Sugar
- Water
- Apple cider vinegar
- Worcestershire and Tabasco
- Salt, pepper and chili flakes to taste

**Recipe**
- Pat the steak dry on all sides. Next, season every side generously with salt and pepper, making sure it’s evenly coated
- Add the oil to a large carbon-steel or cast-iron skillet and turn the heat to high until it smokes lightly. Then, hold the steak with tongs and sear the fat cap for 30 seconds
- Then, turn the steak on its side and let it cook for 1 minute
- Move the steak around in a circular motion for 90 seconds to build a rich Maillard crust
- Turn the steak over and let it cook without touching it for 1 minute
- Lower the heat to medium-low and add 2 tablespoons of unsalted butter to the skillet
- Once the butter is melted, baste the steak for 2 to 3 minutes until the top is golden brown. Then, set the steak on a rack to rest for 5 to 6 minutes
- While the steak rests, drain off most of the cooking fat and place the pan back on the burner over low heat, letting it cool for 1 to 2 minutes. Turn the heat to medium and add 1 to 2 tablespoons of butter. Once it’s melted, add the eggs and fry them for 2 to 3 minutes or until they reach preferred doneness. Season the eggs with salt and set them aside
- Place the pan back on the burner over medium heat and cook the minced garlic cloves for 30 to 45 seconds until fragrant
- Add the Worcestershire, vinegar, sugar, pepper flakes, hot sauce, and water, then cook for 1 minute
- Turn off the heat and swirl in 2 to 3 tablespoons of cold unsalted butter until the sauce is smooth and emulsified
- I finish the sauce with green onions and serve it right away
