After four years of college living in this same 156 sq ft room, I finally decided it was time for a makeover and the first big step was getting a loft bed to open up the space. I’ve always dreamed of having a big apartment but before I get there I want to make the most of the space I already have. This room holds a lot of memories and now I’m transforming it into a compact home design studio where I can dream, design, prototype, and build the things I love. I hope you enjoy this transformation, especially the planning part hehe !
